An invited oral presentation during European cystic fibrsosis conference introducing *Achromobacter* spp. bacteria and Danish epidemic strain of *A. ruhlandii*. The talk explores *Achromobacter* spp. role in chronic respiratory infections, and the observed changes in antibiotic resistance over the last 30 years. The presentation explains how genomic research can be utilised to answer scientific questions about bacterial evolution, adaptation, emergence of highly resistant lineages, and what genetic changes are required for the emergence of epidemic strains.
