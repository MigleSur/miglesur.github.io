One DNA read per line. Overlaps are inferred from shared (k−1)-mers.

Beyond the teaching model

How this connects to SPAdes

01

Correct reads

Real reads contain sequencing errors. SPAdes uses coverage patterns and read information to reduce error-derived graph structure before final assembly.

02

Iterate across k

SPAdes constructs and refines graphs through multiple k-mer sizes. Smaller k helps preserve connectivity; larger k can distinguish shorter repeats.

03

Simplify carefully

Tips, bubbles, and low-coverage paths may signal errors—but true variants and uneven coverage complicate the decision. A single threshold is only a teaching approximation.

04

Resolve & spell

SPAdes uses graph structure and additional read evidence, including paired-end information when available, to resolve paths and output contigs.

Educational model: exact k-mer counts, a simple coverage filter, and unitig traversal. It does not run SPAdes or predict its exact output.
