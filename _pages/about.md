## About

I’m Migle Gabrielaite, a senior researcher and Marie Skłodowska-Curie postdoc at Vilnius University. I started my academic path studying biochemistry at Vilnius University, then moved into bioinformatics during my Master’s at the University of Copenhagen. I stayed in Copenhagen for my PhD at Rigshospitalet, where I focused on the genomics of chronic bacterial and viral infections. Afterwards, I spent several years in industry at VUGENE as a bioinformatician and Chief Scientific Officer, working across genomics and broader multi-omics analysis.

My research now centres on how bacteria evolve, adapt, and spread in both the environment and chronic infections. I use microbial genomics, phylogenetics, and computational analysis to uncover how opportunistic pathogens such as Achromobacter spp. transmit, persist, and interact with their hosts. I’m driven by the idea that better genomics can lead to better understanding of emerging opportunistic bacteria which ultimately leads to better prevention, novel therapeutics and better care for people living with long-term infections.
