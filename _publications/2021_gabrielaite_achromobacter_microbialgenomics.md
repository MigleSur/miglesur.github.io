*Achromobacter* species chronically infect the lungs of cystic fibrosis patients, where they undergo genetic adaptation over time. By analysing longitudinal clinical isolates, this study identifies key mutations, gene gains, and losses associated with persistence, antibiotic resistance, and niche adaptation, highlighting mechanisms of bacterial evolution in chronic infections.
