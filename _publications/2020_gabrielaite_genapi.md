GenAPI is designed to identify gene presence and absence in fragmented bacterial genomes, which are common in draft assemblies. The tool accounts for partial genes and assembly gaps, providing robust gene content comparisons across bacterial populations. This enables studies of bacterial adaptation, evolution, and pangenome dynamics in datasets with incomplete genomes.
