We investigated the evolutionary history and genomic features underlying the emergence of the Danish epidemic strain (DES) of *Achromobacter ruhlandii*. By combining longitudinal genome and antibiotic susceptibility data with comparative phylogenomics and genome-wide association analyses, we show that DES emerged around 1990 and acquired genomic features associated with epidemic persistence, including mobile genetic elements, resistance determinants, and enhanced iron acquisition. We further identify additional epidemic *A. ruhlandii* lineages with evidence of long-term persistence and international spread, revealing shared genomic signatures associated with epidemic potential.
