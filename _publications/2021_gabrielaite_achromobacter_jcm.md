*Achromobacter* spp. infections in cystic fibrosis patients pose challenges due to patient-to-patient transmission and emerging antibiotic resistance. This study combines genomic and clinical analyses to track strain spread, identify resistance mechanisms, and inform infection control and treatment strategies.
